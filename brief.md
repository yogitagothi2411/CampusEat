What our CampusEats is doing?

CampusEats is a campus food ordering and delivery system at our college campus specially system for students. It allows students to discover restaurants and menu items, check item availablity, prices, foods they like to eat, add food to cart, place orders, make payments, and track deliveries. The system is designed for students convenience. The system is divided into several services that work together like Account, Order, Payment, Delivery, Catalogue, and Notification services and also trying to introduced the food and restaurants review services, complain services that the student who is ordering get the honest review about the food and restaurants.
The Accoutn Service manages student identity, delivery information such as login credentials, student profiles, delivery addresses. The Catalogue Service provides information about restaurants, menus, prices, and item availablity. The Order Service manages carts and the complete order lifecycle. The payment Service handles payments, payment status, payment methods, and refunds. The Delivery Service managers riders, delivery assigments, and delivery status. The Notification Service communicates order and delivery updates to students.
Overall, CampusEats connects these services so that a student can go from finding food to receiving the order through one system.


Who will use our campusEats System?

The main users of CampusEats are students who wants to order food from campus restaurants.
The system also involves different services and their responsibilities:
Account Service- manages student accounts, profiles, and addresses.
Catalogue Services- manages restaurants, menus, food items, prices, and availability.
Order Service- manages carts, orders, and order status.
Payment Service- manages transactions, payment status, and refunds.
Delivery Service- manages riders, assignments, and delivery status.
Notifiaction Service- sends order and delivery updates to students.

Summary of the CampusEats Service System.

CampusEats is a service-based campus food ordering system in which students can *authenticate, browse restaurants and menus, check food availability, add items to a cart, place and pay for orders, receive deliveries, and get notifications*. The different services communicate with each other through defined operations or contracts. For example, the Order Service can validate a student's address through the Account Service, check food availability through the Catalogue Service, charge the payment through the Payment Service, assign a rider through the Delivery Service, and send updates through the Notification Service.
